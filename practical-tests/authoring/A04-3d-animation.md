# A04 — 3D asset with three animations

**UC:** VIS-001 → VIS-002 → VIS-004. **Work area:** Workfiles run output/A04/ only.

## Brief
Create an original low-poly three-legged scanner drone with a tilting sensor head. Clips: Idle (1.0 seconds, subtle bob, loop), Scan (2.0 seconds, sensor smoothly sweeps and returns, loop), Activate (0.75 seconds, head rises and settles, one-shot). Keep body and legs visually coherent and upright; no unexplained snap or detached part. Do not resubmit the existing rotor beacon.

## Deliver and check
Editable .blend, animated importable .glb, Godot 4.7 preview, correct named clips/loop modes, chronological runtime PNG frames plus useful front/side/perspective viewpoints. Check motion timing, visible head and leg coherence, loop boundaries and one-shot completion. Provide brief, test commands and frame/time-based visual review. Do not claim physics or controller acceptance without exercising them.
