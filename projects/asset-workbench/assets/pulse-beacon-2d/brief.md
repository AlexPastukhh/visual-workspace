# Moving 2D beacon

An editable amber diamond with a dark inset moves horizontally across a neutral background.
The Pulse clip lasts two seconds and loops. At 0 s the diamond is left of centre,
at 1 s right of centre, and at 2 s back at its initial position. Its shape and colour
stay unchanged. The symmetric shape has no required facing direction.

Check chronological motion, a continuous loop, readable silhouette and uncropped framing.
This example exercises the shared 2D stage and standard clip sampler, without custom QA code.
