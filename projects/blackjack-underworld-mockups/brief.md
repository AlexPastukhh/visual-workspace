# UI Sketch Brief

## Purpose
Explore the minimum screen composition for GAME DESIGN — PROTOTYPE v0.3.

## Visual direction
Dark, restrained wireframe. Thin light borders, readable text, neutral card faces,
small restrained status accents. This is a layout sketch, not final art direction.

## Required composition
- Roster screen: six people; choose three or four.
- Shared game table: one screen for both gameplay layers.
- Human players remain in one horizontal row along the bottom.
- Each person has an icon, their Blackjack cards, and decision/outcome text beside it.
- Dealer cards and status are shown separately.
- Underworld hand occupies the central area; card source remains visible.

## Acceptance
- Three distinct readable states render at 1600×900.
- The same human lineup is visible in Blackjack and Underworld states.
- The central hand and lower human row are visually distinct.
- All source is editable in this Godot project; no AI-generated imagery is used.
